What it is

A VPN edge you own, not an app you rent

ForgeEdge deploys a VLESS proxy to a Cloudflare Worker on your account. Your traffic rides Cloudflare's CDN, so it looks like ordinary web traffic.

Yours, on your account

The Worker lives on your Cloudflare account — not a shared server everyone else is on. You hold the keys; nobody can hand your edge to someone else.

Built for censorship

Clean-IP fronting, SNI camouflage, TLS fragmentation, WARP and AmneziaWG — the tricks that keep working when 443 gets throttled.

Zero maintenance

No VPS to rent, patch, or watch. Cloudflare runs the Worker for free on its global network. You just deploy and share the link.

Installation wizard

Live in four steps

The easiest path is the Telegram bot — it does the Cloudflare work for you. Open @Forgeedgebot and follow along.

1

Connect your Cloudflare account

Make an API token at dash.cloudflare.com → My Profile → API Tokens with Workers Scripts·Edit, Workers KV·Edit and Account Settings·Read. Send it to the bot — it verifies, stores it encrypted, and deletes your message.

/cf YOUR_CLOUDFLARE_TOKEN
2

Deploy your edge

One command. In about 30 seconds the bot creates the Worker, its storage, and a public address — then hands you the subscription link.

/deploy
3

Add clean IPs for Iran

Front the edge through clean Cloudflare IPs and a camouflage SNI so it stays reachable when the workers.dev name is blocked.

/addip 188.114.96.3 188.114.97.3 /sni www.speedtest.net
4

Import to your app

Send /sub to get your links, then paste the subscription URL into v2rayNG, Hiddify or Streisand. Configs appear — connect and you're through.

/sub

FAQ

Good questions

Is it really free?
Yes. Cloudflare's free Workers plan is enough to run a personal edge, and the bot and this site cost nothing. You only ever use your own Cloudflare account.
Does it work from Iran?
That's what it's built for. The workers.dev name alone is often blocked, so you front the edge through clean Cloudflare IPs and a camouflage SNI (steps 3) — plus fragmentation and WARP when you need them.
Is my Cloudflare token safe?
The bot verifies your token, stores it encrypted, and deletes your message immediately. It's only ever used to deploy to your own account. Make a scoped token (Workers only) so it can't touch anything else.
How is this different from a VPN app?
A VPN app connects you to someone else's servers. ForgeEdge builds you your own edge on Cloudflare — no shared IPs to get blocked, no subscription to expire, no trust in a provider. You import the config into the same apps you already use.
Why not deploy right here on this page?
Cloudflare's API blocks calls from web pages (CORS), so a static site can't deploy on its own. The Telegram bot does the deploy server-side for you — which is why it's the easiest path.
